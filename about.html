About Julian

I'm fascinated by people. I always have been. Over the years, I've worked with business owners, leaders, teams, parents, young people, and individuals from all walks of life. While every situation is different, I've found that the challenges people face often have something in common.

They come down to the way we think, the value we assign to things, the way we communicate, and the stories we believe about situations, ourselves, and others.

My work helps people understand those patterns, challenge what no longer serves them, and develop practical, ethical, and optional ways of thinking and communicating that create lasting change.

When people understand themselves better, they communicate and operate more effectively. When they communicate better, relationships improve and time becomes an important factor. When time is used wisely, we start managing better, and performance follows.

Things start looking different when there is a focus on what drives us as humans. Energy, fulfilment, and purpose become inevitable. When people see themselves, their relationships, and their challenges differently, they begin making different decisions, and those decisions create different outcomes.

Whether I'm working with a leadership team, a family, a school, a sports club, or an individual, my goal is always the same: to help people think with greater clarity, communicate with greater purpose, focus on alternatives, and strive for the most rewarding and ethical outcome.

I believe people don't resist change, they resist being changed. My role is to create the conditions, the clarity, and the tools for people to choose growth and, more importantly, something meaningful for themselves.

What I believe

  • People are the strategy, not the process around them.
  • Behaviour change is a skill, not a personality trait.
  • Communication is the foundation of every relationship, professional or personal.
  • Growth happens in the same places for a business, a family, and an athlete.
