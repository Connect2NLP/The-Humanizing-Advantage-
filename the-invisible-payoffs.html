The Hidden Need

What the Problem Is Quietly Giving You

Image placeholder

It is rarely because they don't want a better life.

Sometimes, the current situation is quietly meeting another need.

Perhaps it offers certainty.
Perhaps it avoids disappointment.
Perhaps it provides sympathy, familiarity, identity or a reason not to take a risk.

In behavioural psychology and NLP, this is often referred to as secondary gain, the hidden benefit we receive from maintaining a behaviour or situation we consciously say we want to change.

Understanding those hidden payoffs is not about blame.
It is about awareness.

Because once you understand why, and more importantly how, you are holding on, we can begin choosing something better with honesty, intention and purpose.

What is your situation quietly giving you, and what would it take to choose something better?

If you're ready to understand the hidden payoffs keeping you where you are, and to start choosing change with honesty, intention and purpose, I'd be glad to help you take that first step.
