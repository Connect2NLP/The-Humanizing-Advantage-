The Best of Both Worlds

Aligned by Purpose

For more than fifteen years I've had the opportunity to contribute to internationally recognised professional development through Connect2, while building my own vision through The Humanizing Advantage Co. Although these are two distinct organisations, they share a common purpose: helping people understand themselves and others more deeply.

A Journey That Continues

I've proudly worked with Connect2 for more than fifteen years.

It's an internationally recognised organisation specialising in globally recognised certification programmes in Neuro-Linguistic Programming, coaching, leadership and human behaviour.

Delegates join these programmes from around the world, drawn by the same thing that drew me in originally: practical learning, ethical development and qualifications that mean something wherever you take them.

My Role at Connect2

Today, my responsibilities include delivering internationally recognised certification training and, as a certified Connect2 Trainer, assessing and certifying practitioners.

I support delegates throughout their learning journey, help oversee our global programme logistics, and contribute to international marketing.

What matters most to me isn't the list of responsibilities. It's what they've given me: the chance to work with people from diverse industries, professions and cultures, and to keep learning, again and again, how differently people think, communicate and grow.

Learning Along the Way

One of the genuine highlights of this journey has been working alongside Simon Gledhill over many years, training together, facilitating programmes and collaborating on the development of delegates around the world.

Not as a credential I'd point to, but as a colleague who has helped me keep learning while I taught. Those years reinforced principles I try to carry into everything I do:

Integrity. Ethical practice. Curiosity.

Respect. Professional excellence. Human understanding.

The Humanizing Advantage Co.

As my own journey evolved, so did my vision.

The Humanizing Advantage Co. is my personal platform, the space where I work more closely with individuals, leaders, businesses, schools, sports organisations and families.

It isn't built around certification. It's built around practical application, real conversations that centre on leadership, communication, behaviour, relationships, performance, personal growth and human potential.

Why This Benefits My Clients

Some people simply want personalised support. Others want an internationally recognised qualification. Some want both.

Because these two worlds sit side by side in my own work, clients can move naturally from practical coaching into professional certification whenever it aligns with their goals, without starting over somewhere new.

Looking Ahead

I'm proud to still be contributing to Connect2 after more than fifteen years, while continuing to grow The Humanizing Advantage Co.

I've never viewed these as competing journeys. I've always seen them as complementary pathways, connected by one shared purpose:

Helping people think better. Communicate better. Lead better. Live better.

Education expands our knowledge.
Application gives that knowledge meaning.
My passion is helping people experience both.
