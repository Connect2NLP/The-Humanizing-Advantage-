About

Not a CV. A philosophy.

I'm Julian Van Reenen. I trained as an NLP Practitioner, but what I actually do is help people understand behaviour, communication, and thinking clearly enough to change them.

Every problem I've been asked to solve, in boardrooms, classrooms, locker rooms, and living rooms, comes back to the same thing: how people think, how people communicate, and how people grow. That's the work. Not a technique. Not a script. A way of seeing people clearly, and helping them become who they're capable of being.

I believe people don't resist change, they resist being changed. My role is to create the conditions, the clarity, and the tools for people to choose growth for themselves, whether that's a CEO, a parent, a teenager, a team, or a couple.

What I believe

  • People are the strategy, not the process around them.
  • Behaviour change is a skill, not a personality trait.
  • Communication is the foundation of every relationship, professional or personal.
  • Growth happens in the same places for a business, a family, and an athlete.
